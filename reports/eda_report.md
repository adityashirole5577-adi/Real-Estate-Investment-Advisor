## EDA findings

These are observations from this file, not claims about the real housing market:

- Prices range from 10.00 to 500.00 lakhs. The distribution is bounded and should not automatically be interpreted as a natural market distribution.
- The median prices by property type, from highest to lowest, are: Independent House (255.04 lakhs), Apartment (254.02 lakhs), Villa (252.53 lakhs).
- The state with the highest observed average `Price_per_SqFt` is **Karnataka** (0.133 lakhs/sq ft); the lowest is **Punjab** (0.129 lakhs/sq ft). These are file-level comparisons, not validated market rankings.
- Size and price have a Pearson correlation of **-0.003** in this file. Correlation does not establish causation.
- Median prices by BHK are: 1 BHK (254.06 lakhs), 2 BHK (253.30 lakhs), 3 BHK (253.13 lakhs), 4 BHK (254.92 lakhs), 5 BHK (253.87 lakhs).
- Property age and price have a Pearson correlation of **-0.003**. Because age is derived from the same year relationship in every row, this should be interpreted cautiously.
- The feature-availability chart summarizes categorical counts for furnishing, transport access, parking, security, and availability status. These are availability patterns in the file, not measured service quality.
